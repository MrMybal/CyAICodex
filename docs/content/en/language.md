## Language, keyboard and printing {#content}


1. Choose English or Français in FR / EN, or Settings > App language.
2. The application reloads and saves the choice. Newly opened help and tray menu follow it; the assistant is asked to reply in that language.
3. Navigate the manual with Tab and Enter. Diagrams have textual descriptions; screenshots have captions. Print page opens your browser’s print dialog for the selected chapter.

Without a saved choice, English is used. Updates preserve French preferences. Skills, projects, categories, image descriptions and unknown provider messages are not translated automatically. The GitHub starter exists in both languages.

In the standalone manual, its EN / FR selector changes that view only, not your saved application preference.
