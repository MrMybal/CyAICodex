## Choose the interface language {#content}


1. Choose English or Français in FR / EN, or Settings > App language.
2. The application reloads and saves the choice. Newly opened help and tray menu follow it; the assistant is asked to reply in that language.
3. Navigate the manual with Tab and Enter. Diagrams have textual descriptions; screenshots have captions. Print page opens your browser’s print dialog for the selected chapter.

Without a saved choice, English is used. Updates preserve French preferences. Skills, projects, categories, image descriptions and unknown provider messages are not translated automatically. The GitHub starter exists in both languages.

In the standalone manual, its EN / FR selector changes that view only, not your saved application preference.

## Find and read a chapter {#reader}

- Use the search field in the header or press **Ctrl+K**. Search spans this manual in the selected language. Select a result to open its chapter.
- Expand a topic in the sidebar to see its child pages. Hover over or focus a title to read its full name and description.
- Choose a theme from the theme selector. **My space** holds bookmarks and personal notes in this browser.
- The reader remembers reading and sidebar positions when browser storage is available. Clearing browser data or moving the manual can reset these preferences.
- Image controls appear on hover or keyboard focus. Enlarge within the reader, zoom, then press **Escape** to return. No system fullscreen is needed.
